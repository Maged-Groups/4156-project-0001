Everything you need

Four pillars that keep your design system consistent, fast and accessible.

Blazing fast

A build pipeline measured in milliseconds. Hot reload, tree-shaking and zero-config optimisation out of the box.

Modular by default

Every component is a self-contained block you can copy, remix or delete without breaking anything else on the page.

Accessible

Keyboard-friendly focus states, semantic markup and colour contrast that passes WCAG AA without extra effort.

Built for teams

Shared tokens, shared components, shared vocabulary. Onboard a new developer in an afternoon instead of a week.

Why teams choose Nimbus

Designed for humans

Good interfaces disappear. We obsess over rhythm, spacing and contrast so that your users notice the content — never the layout. Every default has been chosen for you by someone who cares.

Engineered for scale

Start with a single landing page and grow into a full product suite without rewriting a thing. Design tokens cascade down, so one change updates every screen in your application at once.

Open by principle

No lock-in, no black boxes. The output is plain HTML and CSS that you can read, edit and hand over to anyone. If you ever want to leave, your codebase stays exactly where it is — yours.
